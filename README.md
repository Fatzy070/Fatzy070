### Parvis

**Learning platform with AI-assisted learning, subscriptions and course management.**

It includes features such as AI-generated lesson summaries and quizzes, course enrollment, certificates, subscriptions and payments, study engagement tools, notifications, support workflows and administrative course management.

### Papertrail

**Browser-based PDF editing and document management platform.**

Papertrail brings document editing directly into the browser, including PDF rendering, text and image editing, document manipulation, export, OCR-assisted workflows and document management.

Building it involves working beyond conventional CRUD interfaces, particularly around PDF coordinate systems, rendering, document processing and browser-based editing.

**Live:** https://papertrail.parvis.com.ng  
**GitHub:** https://github.com/Fatzy070/papertrail-website
